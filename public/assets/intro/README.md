# QuickBite Intro Video Specifications

Place your Google Flow animated food-delivery intro video here:
- `quickbite-intro.mp4`

Requirements:
- Web-optimized MP4 format (H.264 video, AAC audio or muted)
- Recommended resolution: 1080p (1920x1080) or 4K
- The welcome modal autoplays muted and inline, with instant Skip Intro accessibility.
